Changed
~~~~~~~

- The ``authlib.oauth2.rfc7523.JWTBearerTokenValidator`` and the
  ``authlib.oauth2.rfc9068.JWTBearerTokenValidator`` now share a common
  core, parameterized by validation policies. The RFC 9068 validator
  reports a wrong ``typ`` header with a dedicated error description,
  separately from missing claims.

Deprecated
~~~~~~~~~~

- The ``authlib.oauth2.rfc7523.JWTBearerTokenValidator`` now checks the
  ``at+jwt`` ``typ`` header and the ``jti`` claim of the JWT access tokens
  it validates. Tokens that fail those checks are still accepted for now,
  but emit an ``AuthlibDeprecationWarning``. They will be rejected in
  version 2.0.0.
