Added
~~~~~

- OpenID Connect Relying Parties can receive back-channel logout
  notifications. ``validate_logout_token`` checks the signature, ``iss``,
  ``aud``, ``iat``, ``exp``, the back-channel logout event, and rejects a
  ``nonce`` claim. ``BackchannelLogoutReceiver`` also consumes the ``jti``
  claim and destroys the matching session. The Flask, Django and Starlette
  client integrations expose a ``backchannel_logout`` method for the
  ``backchannel_logout_uri`` endpoint. This endpoint is called directly by
  the OpenID Provider and does not use CSRF protection.
